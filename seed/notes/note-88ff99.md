Every shaped pitch cuts something on purpose and writes it under `## For later`.
The idea was to collect those sections into one page per cycle, so the deliberate
cuts are somewhere you would actually meet them at the next betting table instead
of three records down inside a pitch that shipped two cycles ago.

Dropped at the cycle-37 table, for two reasons and both of them are fair.

The sections are already searchable, and the thing anybody wants from them is
"what did we cut from the burner work", which is a search and not a page.

And the harder one: a bin nobody empties is a graveyard with a friendlier name.
For-later items are cut with a reason attached, and the reason usually keeps
holding. Re-reading them every six weeks would mean re-deciding them every six
weeks, which is the cost the cut was supposed to buy back.

Kept here rather than deleted because it is the second time it has been proposed.
