## Notes from the betting table

**The two that cannot slip, and why.** Deterministic means and the bed-solver port are both
standing under other people's work: the validation campaign cannot start until sums stop moving
between runs, and three downstream pitches read the bed solver's interface. Everything else in this
cycle can be stopped on a Wednesday and lose only what it has already spent.

**`pitch-0f0001` — the scan operator on the hearth backend — was bet late in the meeting.** The
argument against it was that the backend work and the port work compete for the same reviewers. The
argument for it, which won, was that we have been describing a GPU backend for two cycles without
ever having lowered one operator onto it, and a description nobody has run is not a plan. It is bet
at five person-weeks across Firecrest and Siskin, with the lowering and the benchmark split so the
second can start before the first is signed off.

**`pitch-0f0003`, the tap-deck rewrite, was shelved rather than dropped.** Nobody argued it was the
wrong thing to do; the room could not name a month in which it was the most useful thing to do.
It is on the shelf with its one task, and the writer task that was going to follow it is now
sitting behind something shelved — which the checker says out loud, and which is the honest state
of it rather than a tidy one.

**`pitch-0f0002` is still shaping and was not brought to the table.** Chaff optics in the burner is
a real problem with no bounded solution written down yet. It has an owner in the sense that
somebody is thinking about it, and no owner in the sense the plan means, so the fields are empty
and the page shows it as estimated rather than pretending to a date.

**The chore that had nowhere to go.** Retiring the f2py shim is a day of work that belongs to no
pitch and no project, and the betting table has no row for that. It was bet as a task in its own
right — `task-0f1005`, parented on nothing — because the alternative was hanging it under a pitch
it has nothing to do with, and a plan that lies about what work belongs to what is worse than a
plan with a loose end in it. `issue-c9d0e1` is the complaint about the table, kept separate from
the chore itself.

**Availability.** Jackdaw is at a half because of the review load, Oxpecker at 0.4 because of the
support rota, and Hoopoe is the only person here for the whole build. Capacity is that fraction
times the build weeks, and the build weeks already know about the holidays — so nobody has to
remember, at the table, that this cycle runs clean through September with nothing taken out of it.
